# Assumptions

Every modeling choice for the fund waterfall, with its default.
- **F1 to F18** are the rules in `SPEC.md`, settled with Jordan on 2026-10-12 (S0).
- **F19 to F21** are the LPs' rules in `SPEC.md`, settled with Jordan on 2026-10-18 for 0.2.0 (S1).
- **F22 to F28** are the classes' and later closes' rules in `SPEC.md`, settled with Jordan on 2026-10-23 for 0.3.0 (S2).
- **F29 to F32** are the management fees' rules in `SPEC.md`, settled with Jordan on 2026-10-25 for 0.4.0 (S3). F29 amends F25 and F28.
- **F33 to F37** are the investments' and carry deal by deal's rules in `SPEC.md`, settled with Jordan on 2026-10-27 for 0.5.0 (S4). F33 widens F4, and F35 amends F5 deal by deal.
- **FA1 onward** are choices the rules don't settle.
- **FC1 onward** are the case-file conventions.

"Term" means it comes from the fund's own terms in the input. "Toggle" means an input field switches it. "Fixed" means there is one behavior. IDs are stable; later steps add to this file and never renumber.

## Management fees (S3)

| ID | Question | Default | Toggle? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F29 | Fee calls at a later close | A fee is each LP's own, on its own commitment by F22's fee weight. A joiner owes its own part of each fee call before its close, as if in from the start: its back fees, paid at its close to the manager. Fees are never rebalanced between LPs. An entered fee call before a later close was paid by the LPs in at its date: its rate per fee weight is the LPs' part over their weights, and every LP's part is that rate times its weight. Every LP's own fee, whatever its close; a period with nothing to call isn't a call. With a schedule no fee call is entered and `fee_rate` isn't given. | FA1 | Does the GP's catch-up count as carry in the clawback test (F12)? | Yes. The carry paid to date is the GP's catch-up plus its carry by step, and the entitlement is the same two from the run on the fund's totals. A catch-up is carry paid early: leaving it out would let a GP keep a catch-up the fund's totals don't give it. ## Added in f01c

| ID | Question | Default | Toggle? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FA15 | How close is the same point (F15) | Within 10^-18 of a dollar. Two breakpoints that close are one, with all their reasons. A tier smaller than that has nothing to pay, so it has no end of its own (FA7). Carry paid less entitlement that close to zero is zero. At 40 digits, two ways of working out one exact amount agree to about 10^-30. Two different breakpoints are fractions whose difference is at least 1 over the product of their denominators, so they can't come within 10^-18 while that product is under 10^18. Spillpoint's engine uses 10^-12 for its ties (E14); this is finer, since nothing here is a choice between equals. | Fixed | Confirmed (f01c review) |

## Known edges

Places where the engine and the reference can differ on an input both refuse, and an input whose result reads ambiguously, recorded rather than changed.

**Keys that look like whole numbers (FA2, FA18; f02b).** `JSON.parse` lists such keys first in a parsed object, whatever their place in the file, so the engine never sees the file's order for them. With two unsupported keys, the later one like "2", the engine names "2" and the reference names the first in the file. Under `terms` this has been so since 0.1.0; under an LP it's new in 0.2.0. Both refuse the input.

| Input | Engine | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| `terms: {"carry": "0.2", "hurdle_irr": "x", "7": "y"}` | `7` at `terms.7` | `hurdle_irr` at `terms.hurdle_irr` |
| an LP `{"name": "A", "commitment": "1", "carry": "x", "2": "y"}` | `2` at `lps[0].2` | `carry` at `lps[0].carry` |
| a class `{"name": "A", "pref": "x", "3": "y"}` (from 0.3.0, f03b) | `3` at `terms.lp_classes[0].3` | `pref` at `terms.lp_classes[0].pref` |
| a schedule `{"start": "2021-01-01", "steps": [{"rate": "0.02"}], "offset": "x", "4": "y"}` (from 0.4.0, f04b) | `4` at `terms.management_fee.4` | `offset` at `terms.management_fee.offset` |

**A class named "Fund terms" (FA28; f03b).** F28 doesn't refuse it. With two or more accounts its reasons read like the fund terms' own: "Fund terms: LPs have their $4,500,000 of paid-in capital back" for each. Every reason and carry step has `class`, null for the fund's terms and "Fund terms" for the class, so code tells them apart; the words alone don't. The page refuses the name, typed or in a file (f03c review, answer 3; f03d), since its picker, its paste and its cards couldn't tell the class from the fund's terms; the engine still takes it.

**A result's `D` values can be the engine's own (f04b review).** What `splitAt`, `splitAtNav`, `findBreakpoints`, `equalization` and `feeCalls` return is fresh each time, every object and list in it, so a caller's edits to them change no later answer. The `D` values in them needn't be: `splitAt` can hand back the engine's own zero. decimal.js documents a Decimal's properties as read-only, so writing into one, as `split.gpCommitmentPaidIn.d[0] = 7` does, is unsupported, and it can change later answers, on any fund. Copying or freezing every `D` a result hands back would slow every split for a misuse decimal.js already rules out (Jordan). `feeCalls` happens to copy its own.

## Later

Each rule's later work, as the spec gives it. None is refused today unless F18 says so.

- **Deal by deal (F35):** a pref, and a catch-up, deal by deal, on what's due, from each check's and each fee call's date; unrealized write-downs due before carry (marks below cost); an interim clawback, and carry escrow (a share of each carry payment held back); a distribution of unused capital, not from an investment; checks funded from a credit line, ahead of their calls; fees and expenses by another rule (all of them first, or by how long each investment was held); spillpoint exits feeding the investments' proceeds, after engine 1.0.
- **Where LPs differ, beyond classes and later closes (F19, F22, F25):** defaulted or excused LPs.
- **Classes (F22):** a class's own pref or catch-up; a fee break that isn't proportional (a different step-down, a fee offset); a class's own expenses.
- **Later closes (F25):** a close after a distribution; a fund that keeps a joining LP's catch-up to reduce its next call; equalization interest treated as capital; the GP commitment in the equalization.
- **Fee calls at a later close (F29):** interest on back fees; back fees paid to the fund and shared between the LPs already in.
- **The management fee schedule (F30):** fees on invested capital, gross or net of exits and write-offs; anniversary periods; fees in arrears; fee offsets and waivers; a fee step that isn't proportional for a class (F22's Later); an LP's own fee start.
- **Capital in tier 1 (F5):** invested capital only; committed capital.
- **Compounding pref (F7):** per call or as an IRR hurdle, built to a real fund's wording and checked against its model. Refused as `compounded_pref` until then.
- **A step's higher rate on all profit, with a GP catch-up (F11),** since terms vary.
- **Clawback (F12):** an after-tax cap; carry holdback or escrow; interim clawback tests.
- **The same total on different dates (F14),** shown as a table, since timing is a curve, not lines.
